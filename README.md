# Volkswagen Group: Northeast and Southeast Asia Corporate Intelligence Profile

An open-source corporate intelligence profile of Volkswagen Group's operations across Northeast and Southeast Asia.

## Central Finding
Volkswagen's public regulatory record across four independent jurisdictions reveals a consistent organizational pattern: market access has repeatedly taken precedence over compliance integrity. This pattern spans two decades, three categories of misconduct, and three continents. Its most acute expression is in China, where structural dependency on state-owned joint venture partners has constrained both due diligence capability and exit optionality.

## Scope
*   Entity: Volkswagen Group (VOW3, Frankfurt SE)
*   Geographic Focus: Northeast and Southeast Asia (China, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia)
*   Methodology: Strict Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) (no proprietary databases, non-public records, or paid data services were used)

## Files
- `/data` — Corporate Structure, locations, key personnel, and Regulatory History (tabular)
- `/output` — One page Pyramid Principle memo and final exported map
- `/qgis` —  QGIS project file

## Methodology and Sources
Research was structured across four domains, each drawing on a distinct source base:

- Corporate Structure: Volkswagen AG Annual Reports (2024/2025), OpenCorporates, company investor relations portals, SAIC Motor annual reports, and Gotion High-Tech IR pages.
- Geographic Footprint: Company facility announcements and press releases, Google Earth Pro for coordinate verification, QGIS 3.44.7 for spatial mapping.
- Regulatory & Litigation History: Volkswagen AG Annual Reports 2022-2025 (litigation sections), US EPA, US DOJ, US CBP, US Senate Finance Committee (May 2024), NDRC/Hubei Price Bureau, Korea Fair Trade Commission (KFTC), European Commission, and the UK CMA.
- Personnel Intelligence: LinkedIn, Volkswagen Group China press releases, Audi AG press releases, CARIAD press releases and national subsidiary IR pages.
